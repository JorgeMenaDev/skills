# Andy Publicaciones: beat map

- **Product:** Andy Publicaciones, a social scheduler (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok).
- **Request:** a café owner writes one post: "Mañana abrimos en Lastarria ☕ / Café gratis de 8 a 10."
- **Viewer learns, in order:** write once → pick channels → schedule → approve → published everywhere → a whole week planned.
- **Language:** Spanish (Chile). **Brand:** Geist, monochrome UI, the logo's blue gradient (#4BC0F4 → #616FF7) as the one accent.
- **Music:** Mixkit "Rising Forest" (id 471), 124.00 BPM, offset 9.7051 s, breakdown on film beats 8–11, drop on beat 12.
