# Worked example: a B2B product intro (2026-10)

An 87 s Spanish intro film for a contractor-accreditation SaaS, emailed to its first customers the day before
their trial. Two sides use it: the company that hires contractors (the "mandante") and the contractors who
upload their documents. Its scene file is about 1,250 lines; the source stays private with the product's owners.

## How it got there

The first cut followed the feature-launch default: 41 s, every feature squeezed into one take, the step-4 gate
self-checked because of a same-day deadline. The human found it dense, and it was for viewers who had never seen
the product. The second cut became the product intro in `film.md`: same scenes, twice the time, plus an opening
and an ending. Showing the cut and four stills before building would have saved the first round.

## What made the second cut work

- **The landing page's own copy** for the hero (headline, subtitle, industries), so the opening says what the
  company already says.
- **The problem as objects**: five cards (an email thread "RE: RE: F30-1 corregido (v3 final).pdf", a
  spreadsheet "Contratistas_2026_FINAL_2.xlsx", a WhatsApp ask, an expired exam, a stopped permit) that fly into
  the logo.
- **The whole song** (Rising Forest): the features grid on drop 1 (beat 32), the AI document review calm in the
  quiet section (one check every 2 beats), the human approval click on drop 2 (beat 96), the ending on the outro.
- **An honest payoff**: the AI recommends, the customer's reviewer clicks Approve on the drop, then the
  contractor turns «Habilitada». The first cut jumped from the recommendation to the result.
- **A numbered step label** (bottom-left; top-left covered page titles) through the walkthrough.
- **Next steps the viewer takes**: where the invitation arrives, completing a profile, the support address.
