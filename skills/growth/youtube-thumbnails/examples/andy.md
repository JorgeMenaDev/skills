# Worked example: Andy Partner (2026-09)

Channel https://www.youtube.com/@andypartnerapp. Andy is an AI agent for WhatsApp and Instagram for small businesses; Spanish-speaking Latin America, Chile first.

- **Brand kit:** round bird mascot, cyan #3ABCEE → violet #5A4EFB, yellow beak, thick black outline (`A1.png`, transparent). Site andypartner.com: black, fine grid, blue glow, Geist.
- **What failed first:** HTML templates (big white headline + UI screenshot + glow). The owner called them "AI-generated and very sloppy". That rejection is why this skill paints scenes with a model.
- **What worked in the first model test:** a photoreal laundry at night, folded towels, a phone showing "Reserva confirmada" at 23:47, the words "Responde por ti". A real place, a real moment, correct Spanish accents.
- **What drifted:** the mascot passed as a reference lost its outline and changed its eyes. It is composited from `A1.png` instead.

Launch film (31 s: a customer asks a laundry for a price at 23:47; Andy answers and books):
1. *23:47*: a dark laundromat after closing, a phone lighting up with the reply and the booking.
2. *Mientras dormías*: the owner asleep; on the nightstand the phone shows the booking made.
3. *El cliente de las 23:47*: close-up of a customer's hand typing "¿cuánto sale?" late at night.

Tutorial (80 s: create your AI agent in 5 steps):
1. *5 pasos*: a small business owner at the counter, laptop open, a big "5 PASOS", a satisfied look.
2. *Antes / después*: left, a phone flooded with unread chats; right, the same counter calm, chats answered.
3. *Tu turno*: the owner's hands setting up the agent on a laptop, a café or salon behind.
