# Thumbnail craft: what the evidence says

Labels: **[measured]** counted data, **[official]** YouTube documentation, **[creator]** a named operator's own experience, **[opinion]** analyst consensus. Compiled 2026-09.

## Principles

- **Custom thumbnails are table stakes.** 90% of the best-performing videos use one. [official] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300
- **One focal point, three elements at most.** Paddy Galloway's three-element rule; working designers say the same. [creator] https://x.com/PaddyG96/status/1450463472907657220
- **A face, or a mascot with a face, is the differentiator; contrast is hygiene.** vidIQ coded 500 breakout videos: 69% used a face (80% of the top 50), only 5% an exaggerated expression. [measured] https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-thumbnail-study. 1of10's 2025 report finds the face lift is small (~4%) and only above 200K subscribers, so small channels lean on the object, the result or the words. [measured, vendor] 1of10 Data Report 2025. MrBeast's A/B tests moved his archive to closed-mouth faces. [creator] https://www.theverge.com/2023/9/6/23861576/youtube-face-mrbeast-open-closed-mouth-a-b-testing-thumbnail
- **Text: 0–3 words.** 28% of breakouts had no text; 24% had 1–3 words. [measured] vidIQ, above. Tutorials still need a few words so viewers know the video answers their question. [opinion] https://1of10.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-trends-2026
- **Three colours, one extreme.** Cyan, green and yellow lead; cyan-dominant thumbnails ran 36% above average. [measured, vendor] 1of10 2025. Keep clear of YouTube's own red, white and black chrome. [opinion] https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-thumbnail-design-tips
- **Package, not picture.** Title and thumbnail tell one story with near-zero overlap; decide both before shooting. [official + creator] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300, https://www.colinandsamir.com/resources/the-new-rules-of-youtube-from-paddy-galloway
- **Design for the phone.** Suggested-video thumbnails render at 168×94. Paste yours into a real feed screenshot and look. [creator] https://www.businessinsider.com/mrbeast-youtube-thumbnail-strategy-advice-for-creators-2021-10

## What reads as AI slop

1. Garbled small text, waxy skin, broken hands.
2. The default look: centred subject, purple-blue glow, glassy floating UI, a headline over a screenshot.
3. Rich detail that turns to mud at 168 px.
4. No moment: the image names the topic instead of showing someone asking, booking or winning.
5. A mascot that looks slightly different every time.

YouTube made "managing AI slop" a 2026 priority, and about 21% of a new account's first recommendations were measured as slop. https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/dec/27/more-than-20-of-videos-shown-to-new-youtube-users-are-ai-slop-study-finds. Viewers spot it at the thumbnail first. AI thumbnails that win do so through faster iteration (more variants tested), not because one image is better. [opinion] 1of10 2026.

## Channel patterns worth copying

- Official SaaS channels (Supabase, Zapier, Notion, HubSpot) package for **search**: literal titles, product-led images. That only works once people search for you.
- Creator tutorial channels (Kevin Stratvert, Fireship, Thomas Frank, Ali Abdaal, Platzi, midudev) package for **browse**: a face, curiosity, an outcome. A new channel with no search demand starts here.
- Thomas Frank keeps one visual system across a series so the sidebar reads as a set.

## Specs

- 1280×720 minimum export (3840×2160 recommended), 16:9, JPG/PNG/GIF, 2 MB via mobile upload, 50 MB via desktop. Custom thumbnails need a verified channel. [official] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431
- Bottom-right holds the duration badge; the bottom edge holds the watched-progress bar; desktop hover puts buttons top-right; corners are rounded. Keep subject and words in the central ~80%.
- **Test & Compare**: up to 3 thumbnails, winner chosen by watch-time share, results in days to two weeks, desktop Studio only. [official] https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400

## Checklist

1. One focal point; three major elements at most. A before/after pair counts as one unit.
2. The subject shows a clear emotion that still reads at 168×94; on a small channel an object-led result image may stand in for the face.
3. Text is 0–3 designed words, large, every glyph (accents included) correct. Evidence text inside a style profile follows that profile.
4. The words add something the title does not say; a searchable tutorial may repeat its number ("5 PASOS").
5. Three designed colour families at most, one extreme; brand-asset colours, natural skin and photographic neutrals are exempt; the base is not YouTube red/white/black.
6. The subject fills about a third of the frame; the background is simple.
7. The logo or mascot is the real file, or passed the side-by-side identity check.
8. No garbled text, hands or invented brand marks anywhere in the scene.
9. Bottom-right fifth, bottom edge and corners hold nothing important.
10. Passes the phone test in the review sheet's 168×94 column.
11. A stranger gets the promise in under a second.
12. Three diverse concepts reach final/ for the video (different hook, not different hue); a failed concept is rebriefed, never shipped short.
13. Exported 1280×720 JPG under 2 MB.
14. It does not look like the default AI look listed above.
15. Handoff, not a gate: after publishing, the publisher judges by watch-time share after 1–2 weeks, not day-one CTR.
